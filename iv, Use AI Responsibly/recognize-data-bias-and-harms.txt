# Course 4 introduction: Use AI responsibly
  + Responsible AI
    - The principle of developing and using AI ethically, with the intent of benefiting people
      and society while avoiding harm
  + AI user
    - Someone who laverages AI to complete a personal or professional task

# Understand bias in AI
  + Systemic bias
    - A tendency upheld by institutions that favors or disadvantages
  + Data bias
    - A circumstance in which systemic errors or prejudices lead to unfair
      or inaccurate information, resulting in biased outputs.
  => AI models are value laden
