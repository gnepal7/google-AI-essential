  => In prompt engineering, the word 'shot' is often used as a synonyms for the 
    word 'example'
  => 0 shot = no example
  => 1 shot = 1 example
  => Few-shot prompting = A technique that provides two or more examples in a prompt

# Techniques for mastering complex tasks with AI
  + Chain-of-thought prompting
    - technique that involves requesting an LLM to explain its reasoning process 
      step-by-step, from input to final output
    - This simply include key phrases in your prompt, like:
      . 'Explain your reasoning'
      . 'Go step by step'
  + Prompt chaining
    - It helps you tackle large projects by breaking them into a series of smaller, 
      connected steps that are all in the same chat
    - This includes 3 key steps:
      . Task analysis
      . Initial prompting
      . Input/output flow
  + Combining prompt chaining with chain-of-thought
    - Combinations of both prompting techniques may provide qualitative and accurate solution.
  + Limitations and best practices
    - AI tools can struggle to remember context from earlier parts of the conversation as the 
      prompt chain grows longer. This can lead to:
      . Inconsistent responses
      . Overlooking important details from earlier prompts
      . Difficulty maintaining the overall objective of the task
    => To overcome these challenges and ensure effective prompt chaining, consider the 
      following strategies:
      > Use checkpoints
      > Work on sub-tasks
      > Recap and redirect
