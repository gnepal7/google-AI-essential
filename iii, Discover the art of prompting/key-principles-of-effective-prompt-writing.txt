# Prompting best practices
  + Simple framework:
    . Task
    . Context
    . References
    . Evaluate
    . Iterate
  => Specify the Task
      - Persona : What expertise do you want the AI tool to draw from, and who is the audiende?
      - Format : How do you want the output to be formatted? like : bullet list or paragraph, hashtag, list etc.
  => Provide necessary context, might include:
    - Reasons and objectives for performing the Task.
    - Rules or guidelines that the output must follow.
    - Relevant background information the tool should consider.
  => Include references as examples, suggested:
    . Briefly explain how the references relate to the task.
    . Use 2-5 high-qualiti examples that closely align with your needs.
    . Include your own work or open-source examples if relevant.
  => Evaluate your output, focus on factors suah as:
    - Accuracy
    - Bias
    - Relevancy
    - Consistency
  => Iterate for better results, the process typically goes like this:
    - You provide an initial prompt.
    - The AI tool responds with an output.
    - You evaluate the effectiveness of the AI-generated responses.
    - You refine your request based on what worked and what didn't.
    - The process repeats until the AI tool produces the desired results.
