# Understand large language models
  + Large Language Model (LLM)
    - An Ai model that is trained on large amounts of text to identity patterns between
      words, concepts, and phrases so that it can generate responses to prompts.
  + Factors that can contribute to hallucinations
    . Quality of an LLM's training data
    . Phrasing of the prompt
    . Method of LLM uses
