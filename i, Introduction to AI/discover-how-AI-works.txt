# A guide to AI and ML
  + Approaches to machine learning
    1. Supervised learning, is used to train tools from a massive dataset that has been 
      labeled by humans
    2. Unsupervised learning, is not labeled by humans
    3. Reinforcement learning, a process of trial-and-error that is guided by feedback
  
  + Rule-based AI: A different approach
    - Hard-coded rules created by human developers. Eg: chatbots
